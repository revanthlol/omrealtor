Township & Location Guide

Why Ulwe is Navi Mumbai’s Golden Node.

Designed by CIDCO with wide 30-meter avenues and rapid multi-modal transit, Ulwe offers both quality family living and top-tier real estate capital appreciation.

Infrastructure Transformation

The convergence of Sea, Rail, and Air connectivity.

Until recently, travel from Navi Mumbai to South Mumbai took over 80 minutes. With the inauguration of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Sewri–Nhava Sheva Atal Setu (MTHL), residents of Ulwe reach Nariman Point and Sewri in just 20 minutes.

Combined with the operational Kharkopar and Bamandongri railway stations on the Nerul–Uran Harbour line, and the imminent commissioning of the Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA), Ulwe has transformed into one of the most sought-after nodes in Maharashtra.
