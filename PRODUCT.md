## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are homebuyers and tenants evaluating property in Ulwe and the wider Navi Mumbai coastal corridor. Secondary users are residential and commercial investors, landlords, NRI owners, and local businesses seeking shops or offices.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces OM ENTERPRISES, explains its property advisory and transaction support, and converts qualified visitors into direct phone or WhatsApp consultations. Success means a visitor can quickly understand where the firm operates, what help is available, why the firm is credible, and how to speak with Mr. Manoj Kumar.

## Positioning

OM ENTERPRISES is a high-touch, locally rooted Ulwe property advisory led personally by Mr. Manoj Kumar. Its stated mechanism is end-to-end help across discovery, negotiation, document review, registration, leasing, and post-transaction support rather than acting as a generic property-listing portal.

## Operating Context

The firm operates from Sai Nakshatra in Sector 18, Ulwe and serves residential sales, rentals and rental management, commercial property, title and RERA document checks, and home-loan coordination. Visitors commonly compare locations around Kharkopar station, Atal Setu access, Navi Mumbai International Airport, and Ulwe sector amenities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Direct consultation by phone and WhatsApp is the primary conversion path.
- Property advice covers buying, resale, renting, leasing, commercial property, document due diligence, and financing coordination.
- The current repository is a zero-build static web deployment. A framework migration is optional, not required for the redesign.
- No live property inventory, filtering system, CRM integration, or working lead backend is confirmed.
- Property availability, pricing, commute times, review counts, and third-party service relationships may change and must not be presented as live data without a maintained source.

## Product Principles

1. Make local expertise specific and useful, not generic real-estate marketing.
2. Show real places and services before making claims.
3. Keep consultation actions immediate on mobile and desktop.
4. Treat trust information as factual content that must remain verifiable.
5. Never imply a property is available, verified, or endorsed without current evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion preferences, labeled controls, meaningful image alternatives, and responsive layouts from small phones through wide desktops.
