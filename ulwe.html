What to check on a visit.

Property value is only one part of daily fit.

Morning and evening

Visit at realistic travel times. Check noise, traffic, lift use, parking and the walk to transport.

Building records

Ask for the available OC, CC, RERA, society and title documents that apply to the transaction.

Everyday services

Map groceries, healthcare, schools, deliveries, water, power backup and the routes you will use weekly.
